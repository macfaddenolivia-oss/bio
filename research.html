Research & Experience

Selected projects

  • Mapping 40 years of malaria treatment resistance

    Novartis, Global Health Division · 2019–2020

    As part of a clinical trial team for an innovative malaria treatment, I introduced geospatial data analysis to Novartis’ Global Drug Development department, analyzed gene mutations relevant to malaria treatment, and produced hundreds of maps spanning over 40 years of treatment-resistance data.

  • Gaps in drug development for neglected diseases

    Drugs for Neglected Diseases initiative (DNDi) · 2023

    Extracted and analyzed data from the regulatory authorities of more than 30 countries and systematically coded disease data to identify where pharmaceutical R&D falls short of the global disease burden, informing strategic recommendations for future research investments.

  • Desertification, land degradation, drought, and health

    UNDP, for the UN Convention to Combat Desertification · 2024–2025

    An extensive literature review on the connections between desertification, land degradation, drought, and health for the United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD).

  • Climate and diarrhea in sub-Saharan Africa

    Centre Suisse de Recherches Scientifiques en Côte d’Ivoire · 2022

    Guided PhD students in conducting systematic literature reviews on climate-sensitive factors and diarrhea in sub-Saharan Africa, with strategies for comprehensive data collection and analysis.
